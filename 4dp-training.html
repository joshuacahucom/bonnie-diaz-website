What is 4DP?

Partnership in four dimensions

The 4th-Dimension Partnership™ (4DP) is a dance system built on the physical, social, emotional and spiritual dimensions of partnership. It helps dancers restore balance, deepen awareness and strengthen connection with themselves and others, on and off the dance floor.

4DP works for solo dancers, children, adults and professional couples alike.

The story

Why Bonnie created 4DP

On the dance floor, Bonnie experienced many joys. But she noticed that many other couples weren't happy with themselves or each other, and it showed in their dancing. 4DP is her answer.

Read the transcript

Hi, Bonnie Diaz here. As a former U.S. World Mambo Champion, three-time Ten Dance Representative and eleven-time undefeated Basic Latin Champion, I experienced many joys on the dance floor. However, I noticed that many other couples were not having the same experience. They weren't happy with themselves or each other, and it showed while they were dancing.

That's why I created the Fourth Dimension Partnership Dance System. It is based on the physical, social, emotional and spiritual. By implementing the Fourth Dimension, also known as 4DP, you can restore balance, deepen awareness, strengthen connection within yourselves and others, and explore how higher-dimensional thinking can bring clarity, purpose and harmony to your dance of life.

It is my job and my life's purpose to share this with as many people as possible. It can work for a solo person, for a child or an adult, on and off the dance floor, especially with professional dance couples. I am looking forward to presenting this all over the world. Thank you.
