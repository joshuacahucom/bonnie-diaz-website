LIFCON 2026

The 4th Dimension: Restoring Balance & Connection

Bonnie's keynote at LaBlast Fitness's LIFCON 2026, hosted by Dancing With The Stars celebrity Louis van Amstel. It introduces the “4th dimension” as a way to restore balance, deepen awareness and strengthen connection, both within ourselves and with others.

Through movement, discussion and reflection, participants explored how higher-dimensional thinking can bring clarity, purpose and harmony to the dance of life.
